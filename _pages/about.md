**About me**

I am a lecturer at the School of International Studies, University of International Business and Economics (UIBE) in Beijing, China. I hold a PhD degree in Applied Linguistics from the School of Language and Linguistics, Faculty of Arts, University of Melbourne, Australia. My research interests include psycholinguistics, second language acquisition and cognitive linguistics. I am also proficient in quantitative research methods and experiment programming.
